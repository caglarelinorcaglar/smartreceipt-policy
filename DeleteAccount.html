Response Time: We will process your deletion request within 15 days and send you a confirmation email.

What Gets Deleted

  • From Our Servers: Your name, email address, and AI token balance (stored in Firebase)

What About Your Receipts?

Since we never stored your receipts on our servers, there's nothing for us to delete. Your receipts are only on:

  • Your Device: Stored locally on your phone
  • Your Google Drive: If you enabled backup (you can delete these manually)

⚠️ This action cannot be undone! Once we delete your account, you'll lose access to any purchased AI tokens and will need to create a new account to use the app again.

Alternative: Just Uninstall

If you just want to stop using the app but might come back later, simply uninstall the app. Your account data (name, email, token balance) will remain in our servers, and you can sign back in anytime.
