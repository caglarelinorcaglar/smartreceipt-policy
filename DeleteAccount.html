🗑️ Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go! If you'd like to delete your Smart Receipt account and all associated data, please follow the instructions below.

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your receipt data will be permanently deleted.

What Will Be Deleted

  • Your account: Your Google sign-in association with Smart Receipt
  • Local data: All receipts stored on your device (uninstall the app)
  • Cloud backup: Receipts in your Google Drive (you can delete these manually)

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your account, please send us an email with the following information:

  • Your registered email address (the one you use to sign in)
  • Confirmation that you want to delete your account
  • Any feedback (optional)

Contact Us

Send your deletion request to:

📧 Request Account Deletion

Email: receiptvault25@gmail.com

Response Time

We will process your deletion request within 30 days and send you a confirmation email once completed.

Alternative: Delete Data Yourself

You can also delete your data manually:

  • Sign out from the app (Settings → Sign Out)
  • Uninstall the Smart Receipt app from your device
  • Delete Google Drive backups (if you enabled cloud sync)
